WASTE RECEPTACLE DATASET ANNOTATION GUIDELINES
Task: Object Detection with Sub-Classification Attributes (CVAT Setup)
================================================================================
1. CORE INSTRUCTIONS FOR TEAM ANNOTATORS
================================================================================
• Every annotation must map strictly to the core PARENT class 'waste_bin'.
• You must use the properties panel in CVAT to select the required SUB-CLASSIFICATION attribute.
• Color codes must match the parent configuration exactly to ensure workspace consistency.

================================================================================
2. PARENT CLASS TAXONOMY & ATTRIBUTE ASSIGNMENTS


PARENT CLASS: waste_bin [COLOR: BLUE / #0000FF]

Draw a bounding box around every visible container designed for waste, trash, recycling,
or compost collections. Once drawn, select one of the following mandatory
sub-classification attributes based on their structural design and context:

Sub-Class Attributes:
• dumpster         : Large, heavy-duty industrial or commercial waste containers
typically found outdoors in alleys or parking lots (e.g., the
large grey steel dumpster on wheels in the top-left pane).
• sensor_bin       : Modern indoor smart bins with electronic, touchless automated
opening lids (e.g., the stainless steel bin with an open lid
in the top-right office pane).
• outdoor_can      : Slatted, mounted, or heavy-duty bins designed explicitly for public
parks, walkways, and streetscapes (e.g., the green wooden-slat
park bin in the bottom-left pane).
• standard_indoor  : Typical household or utility bins including plastic storage totes,
step-pedal cans, and basic bins (e.g., the blue recycling tote, red
pedal bin, and black utility can in the bottom-right kitchen pane).

================================================================================
3. EDGE CASE RESOLUTIONS & QUALITY STANDARDS

• Tight Fit Margin: Bounding boxes must fit within 2 pixels of the absolute outermost
silhouette of the target receptacle, including any open lids. No loose background padding is permitted.
• Grouped Items: For multiple bins placed directly side-by-side (like the blue, red, and black bins),
draw individual, distinct, tight bounding boxes for each separate receptacle. Do not group them into one big box.
• Structural Shadows: EXCLUDE all ground-cast shadows from the bounding box. Do not stretch boxes to
include dark contact zones on the pavement, grass, or kitchen tiles.
• Grounding & Surroundings: Do not include independent elements like adjacent wooden pillars, desk tables, computers,
or cardboard debris lying on the floor outside the immediate boundary of the container structure.
