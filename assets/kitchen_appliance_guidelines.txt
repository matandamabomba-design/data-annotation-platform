CVAT ANNOTATION GUIDELINE: HOME KITCHEN & APPLIANCES
===================================================
Project Domain: Smart Home Automation & Interior Object Recognition
Task Type: Object Detection (Bounding Boxes / Polygons)

1. OBJECT CLASSES & LABELS
-------------------------
- `microwave`: Countertop or built-in microwave ovens.
- `toaster`: Small electric appliances designed for toasting bread.
- `blender`: Food preparation appliances featuring a glass or plastic pitcher base.
- `chair`: Dining room or kitchen table seating units.
- `mug`: Ceramic or glass beverage cups with handles resting on surfaces.
- `fruit_bowl`: Display bowls containing fresh produce (label the bowl structure itself; fruits can be separate sub-labels if required by your project taxonomy).

2. ANNOTATION INSTRUCTIONS (BOUNDING BOXES)
------------------------------------------
- Structural Completeness: For items like chairs, ensure the box encloses the entire height from the top headrest down to the floor-contact point of the legs.
- Appliance Handles/Cords: Include physical door handles, control knobs, and attached power cables in the appliance bounding box if they project outwards.
- Cluttered Surfaces: When mugs or spice shakers are clustered together, separate them into individual overlapping bounding boxes. Do not lump independent targets together into a single box.

3. SPECIAL CASES IN THIS SCENE
------------------------------
- Glass Reflection: For objects visible through window panes or glossy cabinet surfaces, do not label reflection ghosts. Only annotate physical objects in the room space.
- Partial Invisibility: If a chair is hidden underneath the dining table, draw the box to enclose both the exposed backrest and any visible leg sections below.
