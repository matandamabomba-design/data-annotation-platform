Practice room for beginners

Data annotation practice

Label short texts the way a real project would ask. Read the rules first, then work alone for the full time. Compare with your group afterwards.

Project rules

Choose the feeling of the writer about the topic.

  • Positive The writer is happy, satisfied or praising.
  • Negative The writer is unhappy, frustrated or criticising.
  • Neutral Facts with no feeling, or good and bad points that are equally strong.
  • Sarcasm: label what the writer means, not the words on the screen.
  • All texts are in English.
  • Use only what is written. Do not guess the writer’s situation.

Keys: 1, 2, 3 to label. Left and right arrows to move.

Your result

Review with your group

Where you and a teammate chose differently, read the rules together and decide which label fits. That discussion is the practice.
